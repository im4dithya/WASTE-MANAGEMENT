XYCLO is a comprehensive smart waste management system designed to streamline waste 
collection, recycling, and user interaction for cleaner and more sustainable communities. The 
application addresses the growing challenges of urban waste by providing a centralized digital 
platform that manages the entire process—from user waste requests to pickup, verification, and 
recycling—reducing manual errors and improving transparency. 
The system is divided into four main modules: Admin, User, Pickup, and Recycler. 
The Admin module manages areas and waste types, allocates regions to pickup teams, verifies 
pickups and recycling units, monitors public offences, and handles complaints and feedback to 
ensure efficient system control. The User module allows residents to register, send waste 
requests, view request status, explore waste types, submit complaints and feedback, and purchase 
recycled products through integrated cart and payment features. The Pickup module enables 
collection staff to manage their profiles, view and respond to user waste requests, collect waste, 
update request status, and track their allocated areas. The Recycler module supports recycling 
units in managing profiles, products, orders, payments, and user requests, promoting recycling 
and circular use of materials by digitizing coordination between users, pickup staff, and recycling 
units, XYCLO overcomes the limitations of traditional manual systems that suffer from irregular 
pickups, poor record-keeping, and lack of communication. 
Current waste management systems are largely manual and disorganized, relying on paper-based 
records, irregular pickups without proper scheduling or area allocation, and minimal coordination 
between collectors, recyclers, and authorities. This leads to waste accumulation, poor 
transparency, inefficient verification, limited user interaction for complaints or tracking, and 
environmental pollution due to unmonitored disposal. 
XYCLO introduces a centralized digital platform that automates waste handling from request 
submission to recycling with role-based access for seamless coordination. It enables real-time 
tracking, automated area assignments, verification workflows, and integrated e-commerce for 
recycled goods. The system addresses limitations of existing applications by offering public 
offence reporting, recycler product marketplaces with cart functionality, admin verification 
dashboards, and comprehensive feedback loops.
